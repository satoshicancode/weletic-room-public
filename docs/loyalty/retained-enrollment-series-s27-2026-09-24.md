# S27 retained account enrollment series candidate — September 24, 2026

This is a **partial analytics implementation candidate**, not accepted membership history or installed release evidence. The signed merchant snapshot now counts currently retained Loyalty accounts by recorded `enrolledAt` in each UTC month of an explicitly selected interval. It reports the retained-account count before the selected start, new recorded enrollments within each selected month, and their exact cumulative sum. Boundary months include only the selected instants; quiet months show zero. Inactive retained accounts remain included. Counts are exact decimal strings.

The coverage marker `retained_account_enrollments_only` is material. Erased accounts and absent pre-Weletic history cannot be reconstructed. The series does not show the number who were active at an earlier time, a historic VIP tier, or Shopify's total customer base. A current account enrolled earlier remains in the opening count; a later erasure removes the account from this retained projection. Both dates are required, the selected interval is capped at 366 UTC days, and the service returns no account or shopper identifiers. EN/JA/VI copy and owner-only CSV/JSON explain the limits.

The read uses the existing signed Shopify merchant gateway, actor-derived store scope and repeatable-read transaction. It adds no table, writer or migration. The production account schema has a `(storeId, status)` index but no `(storeId, enrolledAt)` index; query-plan and latency verification on the intended provider remain open. The isolated SQL fixture mirrors the existing store/status index, not a proposed index.
