# S33 recorded VIP tier-change reasons candidate — September 24, 2026

This is a **partial analytics implementation candidate**, not historical tier membership or a complete added/promoted/demoted/removed report. It counts retained `WeleticLoyaltyTierHistory` events by their stored reason in each UTC month of an explicitly selected interval. The six current reasons are threshold reached, bonus promotion, annual downgrade, grace period expired, program activation and manual override. An explicit “other recorded reasons” column preserves future reason values. The total must equal the sum of all reason counts exactly.

The selected interval requires both dates and is capped at 366 UTC days. Boundary months include only selected instants; empty months appear with zero counts. Store scope is derived from the authenticated merchant actor and enforced by joining each event to its loyalty account in that store. The signed snapshot and owner-only CSV/JSON exports contain aggregate count strings, never account, shopper or tier-history identifiers. EN/JA/VI merchant text explains the coverage.

Stored reasons do **not** prove historical direction. A tier's current order can differ from its order at the event, while `fromTierId` can become null after tier deletion. The event also does not reconstruct a tier-member population at a past date. Imported or manual changes remain grouped under their recorded reason. Erasure and missing pre-Weletic history can remove earlier events. A future complete S33 needs preserved direction/temporal membership evidence before it claims added, promoted, demoted or removed members.

No schema migration is introduced.
